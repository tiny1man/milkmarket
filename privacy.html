1. Overview

MilkMarket is a static website that provides a directory of links to content hosted on the Internet Archive. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you visit the website.

The website does not intentionally collect personal information through user accounts, submission forms, or direct messaging features.

2. Hosting and Technical Information

MilkMarket is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may automatically process technical information associated with website visits, including IP addresses, browser information, and other information used for security, reliability, and service operation.

This information may be processed by GitHub in accordance with its own policies. MilkMarket's operator does not control GitHub's internal logging practices or data-retention periods.

For more information, see GitHub's Privacy Statement.

3. Cookies and Tracking

To the best of the operator's knowledge, MilkMarket does not intentionally use analytics services, advertising trackers, or tracking cookies.

However, GitHub and third-party services may process technical information independently of the website's own code.

4. Cryptocurrency Donations

MilkMarket may display a public cryptocurrency wallet address or QR code for voluntary donations. Donations are sent directly to the displayed wallet address. The website does not collect donor names, email addresses, or payment details through a donation form.

Cryptocurrency transactions on public blockchains may be visible to others. Transaction amounts, wallet addresses, and transaction histories may be publicly accessible depending on the cryptocurrency used. Blockchain transactions may also be traceable or linked to individuals through other information.

The operator does not automatically learn a donor's real-world identity merely because a donation is received.

5. External Websites

MilkMarket links to the Internet Archive and potentially other third-party websites. When you follow an external link, the destination website may collect information about your visit under its own privacy policy.

MilkMarket does not control the privacy practices, content, or data-handling practices of external websites.

6. Data Sharing

MilkMarket's operator does not intentionally collect and sell visitors' personal information.

Technical information processed by GitHub or other third-party services is subject to those providers' own policies and practices. Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law.

7. Data Security

Reasonable care is taken in maintaining the website. However, no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

MilkMarket does not intentionally collect personal information from children through accounts or submission forms because the website does not provide those features.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website's features or data-handling practices change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised update date.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the website operator through a contact method provided on the website, if available.
